🏐 Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Here's a guide on how to play volleyball for beginners !!

📖 Basic rules
  • Three Touch Limit (cannot touch the ball more than 3 times)
  • No Double Hits (cannot hit the ball twice in succession)
  • Net Faults (forbidden from touching the net while actively making a play)
  • Foot Faults (the server must stand completely behind the endline)
  • Back-Row Attacks (a player positioned in the back cannot spike the ball from inside the attack line)
